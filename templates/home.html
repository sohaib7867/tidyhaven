for your next idea.

Simple upgrades that make your everyday desk feel considered.

Elevate your screenTame the cablesLeave room to think
The 5-upgrade desk guide+
  1. Monitor riser: choose a height that suits your seated eye level and creates useful storage underneath.
  2. Laptop stand: pair it with a separate keyboard and mouse for comfortable use.
  3. Desk mat: measure your keyboard and mouse area before choosing a size.
  4. Cable tray: mount it at the rear of the desk and leave slack for moving devices.
  5. Charging station: check compatibility with your phone, watch and earbuds.
02 / HOME OFFICE

Make room for calm.

Warm light, natural textures, and one small corner to call your own.

The cozy corner guide+

Place your desk beside the window rather than directly facing glare. Add a warm task lamp, a soft desk mat and a plant where it gets suitable light.

Keep one drawer or basket for daily essentials. Leave the area under your desk clear so your chair and legs can move freely.

Display-worthy figures, manga shelves and subtle touches of your favorite characters.

The anime desk guide+
  1. Pick a palette: borrow two colors from your favorite series and keep the rest simple.
  2. Give figures a home: use a stable shelf or the side of a riser, leaving your working area clear.
  3. Make manga part of the space: a small row with bookends creates a display you can actually read.
  4. Add one focal point: try a framed print or character desk mat rather than filling every surface.
